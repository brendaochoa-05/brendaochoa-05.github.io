Hello! I'm Brenda Ochoa

I am Brenda Ochoa, a San Jose State University student majoring in Studio Practice. Art has always been a central part of my life, and I am passionate about creating digital artwork and designs that convey emotion, tell stories, and explore new ideas. I love experimenting with different techniques, tools, and styles, constantly learning and pushing the boundaries of my creativity.

Through my work, I aim to combine imagination with skill, bringing vibrant concepts to life. Whether I’m designing digital illustrations, exploring color theory, or experimenting with textures and patterns, I enjoy the process as much as the final piece. In my free time, I like to explore various art movements and contemporary design trends, finding inspiration in everything from nature and traditional art forms.

My goal is to create artwork that resonates with others, sparks curiosity, and leaves a lasting impression, while continually refining my skills and discovering new ways to express myself creatively.
